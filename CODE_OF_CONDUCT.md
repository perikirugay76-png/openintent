# Code of Conduct

OpenIntent is an open protocol project. Contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful, and focused on improving the protocol.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good intent while still reviewing ideas rigorously.
- Critique proposals, not people.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on actionable changes.
- Make room for contributors from different languages, regions, and technical backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Repeated off-topic promotion.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or block participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Reports can be sent to the maintainers through the repository's public issue process until a dedicated security or conduct contact is established.
